# Surface brief: home page and storefront

Scope: `/` (home), and the storefront pages that inherit its world (shop, product, design tool, checkout, about, contact, policies). Visitor mode: Persuade. Admin is Operate and only inherits tokens.

Audience and job: a business buyer in Sri Lanka fitting out a workspace, usually on a phone, deciding whether this maker can build the tables they need, at their size, for less. Action: design a table or pick one, then order by card, cash on delivery or WhatsApp. Proof: real workshop photos, real finishes, stated sizes and prices. Constraints: no invented reviews, counts or years; policy links stay in the footer; header offset lives in Layout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the site is the workshop's board-sample book. You choose a table the way you choose a finish: by picking a chip. It refuses the furniture-store hero of a lifestyle photo, a slogan and a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: grounds and surfaces are the real materials: ash-white board, teak, walnut, powder-coat black and white steel. Components are sample chips: rounded-corner slabs with a punched hole and a printed label (name, code, size). Type is a heavy, tight grotesque for names and a plain grotesque for labels; numerals are tabular.

STORY: the visitor sees a real table, picks a finish and it changes, understands these are made to order by the maker, then sizes their own in the 3D designer or opens the catalogue, and sends the order.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-bleed real table photo under a clear header. Lower left: one heavy headline stating the offer and two actions, "Design your table" (primary) and "Shop tables". Lower right: a fanned ring of finish chips; choosing one wipes the photo to that table.

FORM: laminate sample book, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:df0667bbf196645e

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: real delivery fees; registration number; no customer reviews entered yet.
